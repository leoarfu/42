Orientation — What this page is

This is not another lesson. It is the technical encyclopedia behind the MOBILE track, written specifically for engineering interviews in 5G Radio Access Networks and Non-Terrestrial Networks.

Unlike the numbered modules, this document is organized by engineering domain rather than learning sequence. During an interview, you should be able to jump mentally between radio physics, protocol layers, cloud architecture, satellite payloads, and troubleshooting. That is exactly how this page is structured.

OBJECTIVE

Be capable of explaining every concept from first principles without relying on memorized definitions. Interviewers rarely test vocabulary; they test whether you understand why an architecture exists.

How to use this masterclass
