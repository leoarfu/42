COMPLETE ENGINEERING CURRICULUM

How ordinary phones talk to satellites

A first-principles course on Direct-to-Device (D2D), Non-Terrestrial Networks, and the future of mobile connectivity.

This course is written for engineers who already understand technology but not cellular networks. It starts with how a normal mobile phone communicates with towers, then progressively replaces the tower with a satellite until the entire 5G NTN architecture becomes intuitive.

Begin Lesson 1

What you'll actually learn

By the end of this course you should be able to explain, from memory, how an iPhone discovers a satellite, synchronizes with it, compensates for Doppler, authenticates through a terrestrial mobile core, and completes a voice call using 5G NTN.

Prerequisites

No prior cellular knowledge is assumed. The only expected background is general technical literacy: IP networking, basic physics, and curiosity.

Important philosophy: this course intentionally avoids memorizing specifications first. Every protocol will be introduced only after the physical problem it solves has become obvious.
